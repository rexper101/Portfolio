Who's on file

The short version — background, education, and how I ended up building AI systems for fun.

I'm currently finishing my Master's in Computer Applications at DPGU, Pune, after a Bachelor's in the same subject. Somewhere in between, I picked up a habit of taking apart AI tools to see what's actually necessary — and building leaner versions myself.

"If it needs an internet connection to work, I want to know why."

That mindset shaped Nano, my offline desktop assistant, and carried into more conventional ML work — resume screening, classifier comparisons, and a couple of full-stack builds along the way. I like projects where the interesting part is under the hood, not just on the surface.
